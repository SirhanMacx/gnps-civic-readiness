# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be respectful and constructive
- Assume good faith
- Welcome new contributors — especially educators or peer-district staff who may not be everyday OSS contributors
- Disagreement is fine; harassment, personal attacks, and discrimination are not

Report concerns to **civicseal@greatneck.k12.ny.us**.
